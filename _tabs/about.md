<p class="lead">
Cybersecurity student in New Zealand. I work on cloud security — mostly AWS and
Entra ID — and I build tooling that makes security reviews faster and more
repeatable.
</p>

Most of what ends up here is a writeup of something I built or broke: identity
blast-radius analysis, permissions-boundary escapes, and experiments in using
LLMs to do the tedious parts of a cloud posture review without turning into
another noisy scanner.

I care about findings that come with a proof of concept. A report that says
"this role is over-privileged" is a guess; a report that shows the exact call
chain out of a permissions boundary is a finding.
